## Lucille

I write about books, places, markets, and the texture of lived experience — especially the moments when a vague preference becomes a clear judgment.

我长期对这些问题感兴趣：好东西为什么好，什么值得投入时间与钱，人如何形成自己的判断，如何获得更多选择权，以及世界不同地方的人如何生活。

## What belongs here

这个网站保存的不是我的全部生活，而是经过时间以后，仍然值得留下来的文章、项目和判断。

This site is curated, not comprehensive. Notes become articles only after they have survived revision, use, and time.

## Subjects

- **Reading** — books, annotations, ideas worth carrying forward.
- **Travel** — places as a way to study taste and ways of living.
- **Investing** — markets, decision-making, and the discipline of judgment.
- **Experience** — attention, pleasure, difficulty, and what makes a life feel vivid.
